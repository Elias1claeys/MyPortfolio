The revenge of Shinobi

Back in 2024, I recreated the first stage of The Revenge of Shinobi for a school project. We were given a custom C++ game engine and tasked with recreating a 2D game. This was my first major game development project, and it gave me the opportunity to learn how to build a game from the ground up. The game still has a few mistakes, but it was a great learning experience and an important step in my development as a programmer. Looking back, I've improved a lot since then, which makes this project a nice way to see how far I've come.

Ketnet Stad

This project was a prototype developed for Ketnet. The goal was to create a multiplayer experience where children could interact and play together in a fun and engaging way. We designed a city-building game in which players receive a set amount of coins each day to purchase buildings and expand their own island. Players could visit their friends islands and there was also a trading system implemented. During this project, I focused on the multiplayer aspects and learned how to work with Firebase to support these features. I chose Firebase because of its strong support for Unity, making it well-suited for integrating real-time functionality into the game. It allowed us to efficiently retrieve and synchronize player data, enabling smooth interactions between players.

Ragdoll Soccers

This game was developed during a game jam. The themes were “Friendslop,” “Minesweeper,” and “Outgrow Hunger,” and we chose to combine the first two. In this multiplayer game, players control ragdoll characters and try to score as many goals as possible while avoiding bombs thrown by their opponent. The mix of chaotic movement and competitive gameplay created a fun and unpredictable experience. We had a great time developing this project, and it placed 13th out of 36 competitors.

SplaShroom

This game was created for a school project, where we were assigned a random team and challenged to make a co-op party game. We created something inspired by Splatoon, but with our own twist: gnomes and mushrooms. You play as a gnome and compete with your teammates to capture as much terrain as possible by painting the ground with mushrooms. Throughout the game, special mushrooms grow across the map. When activated, they grant power-ups such as a speed boost or a larger paint blast... adding another layer of strategy to the gameplay.
