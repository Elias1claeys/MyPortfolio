SplaShroom

❮ Previous ❯ Next

This game was created for a school project, where we were assigned a random team and challenged to make a co-op party game. We created something inspired by Splatoon, but with our own twist: gnomes and mushrooms. You play as a gnome and compete with your teammates to capture as much terrain as possible by painting the ground with mushrooms. Throughout the game, special mushrooms grow across the map. When activated, they grant power-ups such as a speed boost or a larger paint blast... adding another layer of strategy to the gameplay. During this project I focused on the local multiplayer. Player go from the start scene to the lobby scene. And when they all press the ready button the game starts. I also created the clock and the line indicator for when you throw.
