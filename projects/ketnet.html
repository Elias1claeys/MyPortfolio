Ketnet Stad

❮ Previous ❯ Next

This project was a prototype developed for Ketnet. The goal was to create a multiplayer experience where children could interact and play together in a fun and engaging way. We designed a city-building game in which players receive a set amount of coins each day to purchase buildings and expand their own island. Players could visit their friends islands and there was also a trading system implemented. During this project, I focused on the multiplayer aspects and learned how to work with Firebase to support these features. I chose Firebase because of its strong support for Unity, making it well-suited for integrating real-time functionality into the game. It allowed us to efficiently retrieve and synchronize player data, enabling smooth interactions between players.
